## Downloads

Make your options file with the [Options Builder](https://justeyetee.github.io/valheim-archipelago/options/);
the [player guide](https://justeyetee.github.io/valheim-archipelago/guide/) explains how to play.

## Co-op on one Valheim server

Everyone in the world needs the mod. The first player to connect picks the world's co-op mode:
**Shared slot** (one player connects to Archipelago; the others just play) or **A slot each** (everyone connects
to their own slot, each with their own options file, researches, checks and goal).

Most of this project's code, tools and documentation were written by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, at
JustEyeTee's direction.
