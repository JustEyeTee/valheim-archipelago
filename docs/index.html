Valheim Archipelago

A Valheim mod and APWorld for Archipelago, the multi-game randomizer. Recipes and building pieces are locked behind researches; trophies, biomes, skills, dungeons, farming, brewing and more are checks that send items across the multiworld.

Player guideSetup, goals, every kind of check, keys and commands, co-op. Options BuilderMake your options file, with live counts of checks and items. DownloadsThe mod, the APWorld and the PopTracker pack. SourceCode, changelog, troubleshooting and issues.
  • Eight goal bosses from Eikthyr to Kall Fimbulbringer, with goal conditions you can combine (Relic Hunt, Trophy Hunt, Great Hall, Hildir's Quests...).
  • Logic that knows your era: combat gear, comfort, boss keys and progressive items.
  • In game: a panel of what you can do now (F8), a hint shop at the traders, and co-op with a shared slot or a slot each.

Based on Samupo's Valheim Archipelago Randomizer. Most of this project's code, tools and documentation, this page included, were written by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, at JustEyeTee's direction. Public domain (CC0).
