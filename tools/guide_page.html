Valheim Archipelago Guide

How to play Valheim as part of an Archipelago multiworld randomizer.

Options Builder Downloads Source Troubleshooting

What it is

Recipes and building pieces are locked. You unlock them by receiving researches, which come from the multiworld: your own checks and other players'. Crafting a research at the Research Bench, picking up a trophy and many other things you do in Valheim are checks that send items to you or to other players. You win by finishing your goal.

Getting started

  1. Install BepInEx and Jotunn, then the mod (the release zip, or with a mod manager). Put the .apworld in Archipelago's custom_worlds folder.
  2. Make your options file with the Options Builder and put it in Archipelago's Players folder.
  3. Generate and host the room as usual (Archipelago 0.6.8 or newer).
  4. Connect from the Archipelago window on Valheim's main menu (host, port, slot), then load your world. The window is also in the pause menu, to reconnect mid-game.
  5. Build the Research Bench and an Archipelago Chest (Hammer > Randomizer). Resource bundles, tools, maps and boss drops sent to you arrive in the chest.

Goals

Your goal boss sets how far the game goes: only checks and items up to its era are in the game. By default you win by defeating it. goal_conditions changes or adds to what you have to do; pick any combination, and you win once all are done. /ap goal shows your progress.

Items

  • Researches unlock recipes and pieces; with progressive_items, tiered ones come as progressive items (each Progressive Axes unlocks the next axe tier).
  • Trophy researches give permanent boosts.
  • Boss drops (Hard Antler, Swamp Key, Wishbone...) open the next part of the game without the fight.
  • Altar keys, with boss_altar_keys: a boss's altar takes offerings only once you have its key.
  • Maps: a Map to each boss pins its altar; a Map Fragment uncovers part of the map.
  • Tools, resource bundles and boosts (skill training, blessings), and Forsaken Relics in a Relic Hunt.
  • Traps: ambushes, raids, storms, Floor Is Lava, Butterfingers and more.

Co-op

Each world has a co-op mode, chosen by the first player who connects in it (the connection window's "Co-op (new worlds)").

  • Shared slot: one player connects; the others just join the world with the mod. Everyone's checks count for that one slot.
  • A slot each: every player connects to their own slot and has their own researches, checks, goal and Archipelago Chest in the same world. Pass boss trophies around so everyone picks them up.

Trackers

Universal Tracker works without setup. A PopTracker pack is in each release; it follows your seed's options when it connects. In game, F8 and F9 show what's in logic too.
